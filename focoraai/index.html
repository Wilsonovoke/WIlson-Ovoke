Focora AI

AI-Powered Productivity Experience helping people make the most of their day and live a healthier life.

Overview

Focora AI is a focus and productivity app that helps users build habits, stay focused, and move toward their goals with support from an AI coach.


I led the end to end product design, shaping the core experience from structure to interaction.

Role

Lead Product Designer

Scope

  • Visual Design

  • Interaction Design

  • AI Integration

  • Branding

Timeline

3 Weeks

Problem

Most productivity apps overwhelm users with complex dashboards and rigid systems, causing drop off within days.


User research on individuals aged 18-35 revealed the following key statistics:

Goal

Design a calm, motivating experience that helps users understand their day in seconds.

The success metric was simple: reduce cognitive load and increase daily task engagement.

Solution

I designed the home screen as a daily command center that surfaces progress, tasks, and focus guidance in one view.

Users can now understand their status in under 5 seconds and start a task in one tap.

Outcome

The design turned the app into a daily habit rather than a dashboard users avoided.

Faster Daily Clarity

Users can instantly see their progress, priorities, and focus for the day without navigating multiple screens.

Higher Action and Consistency

Clear task structure, one-tap actions, and gentle AI guidance improved follow through and daily engagement.

Stronger Emotional Engagement

Supportive language and contextual suggestions helped users feel encouraged instead of pressured.

This project reinforced that productivity is emotional, not just functional.

Designing with clarity and empathy created a calmer experience that users returned to daily.
