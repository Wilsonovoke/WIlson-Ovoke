GasPaddy

B2C mobile app designed to make cooking gas refills fast, reliable, and rewarding.

Overview

GasPaddy is a mobile-first platform designed to make cooking gas refills fast, reliable, and rewarding. Beyond just reordering, GasPaddy introduces social sharing and a token-based loyalty system (PaddyCoin), creating a seamless blend of utility and community.

Role

Lead Product Designer

Scope

Visual Design

Interaction Design

AI Integration

Branding

Timeline

4 Weeks

Problem

In many communities, ordering cooking gas is stressful and unreliable. Users often struggle with:

  • Inconsistent delivery times.

  • Lack of order tracking.

  • No rewards or incentives for loyalty.

  • Manual and repetitive refill processes.

Existing gas refill services are transactional, not engaging or rewarding.

Goal

The goal was to create a clear and easy-to-use payment experience. Users should be able to complete common tasks with minimal effort.

Solution

GasPaddy redefines the cooking gas experience with:

  • Simple Ordering: Quick refill requests in just a few taps.

  • Real-time Tracker: Track delivery progress with live updates.

  • PaddyCoin Rewards: Earn tokens after every 5 purchases, redeemable for discounts or perks.

  • Community Sharing: Send gas refills or PaddyCoins to friends and family easily.

  • Profile Management: View history, manage addresses, and track rewards.

Outcome

GasPaddy has gone beyond being just a gas refill app—it has redefined how users access and manage household energy needs. By combining convenience, real-time tracking, and a rewarding loyalty system (PaddyCoin), the platform has not only streamlined the ordering process but also fostered trust and repeat engagement. The impact is seen in time saved, reduced stress, and increased customer loyalty, while also introducing a community-driven approach to utility services.
